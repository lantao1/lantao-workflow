\section{How Yields May Respond}

Rainfall goes with higher yields in dry districts. The estimate, however, falls by half once district fixed effects enter the regression. As Section~\ref{sec:data} shows, the rainfall series is complete. The yield measure is very close to the official series.

Yields respond to rainfall with a lag. The lag appears because farmers plant after the first rains, harvest several months later, and report output for the season in which the harvest falls, so a dry spell late in one season lowers the yield recorded for the next season in the survey file.
